    Level 4 · Master Master¶

    Goal: operate at the level of a principal real-time firmware engineer — safety-certified kernels, formal schedulability and worst-case execution-time analysis, production firmware architecture, certification workflows, secure and fault-tolerant designs, and long-term maintenance of shipping RTOS products.
